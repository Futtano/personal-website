# What stays after the last page

This is a sample reading note. Replace it with a book, essay, or passage you want to remember.

A useful reading note does not need to summarize everything. Sometimes one question is enough.

## Three prompts for the margins

- What changed the way you looked at something?
- What did you disagree with, and why?
- Where does this idea connect to something you are making?

A shelf becomes more interesting when it carries traces of the person doing the reading.
