# Making room for play

This is a sample entry about the house you are visiting.

A beach, a quiet room, a computer left switched on. This site starts from a simple question: what if a personal homepage was a place you could walk into?

## A memory, not a reconstruction

The house is imaginary. Its shapes, colors, and objects borrow from the feeling of a summer by the sea and the computers of the late ’90s and early 2000s.

There is no score, and nothing to complete. Walk around, look at the kitchen, stop by the bedroom. The writing is waiting on the computer when you are ready.

You can also skip the walk and read directly. A place to play should leave room for people who just want a quiet page.
